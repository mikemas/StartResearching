  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Information found in newspapers can range from life events like birth announcements, engagement and marriage notices, or obituaries to tales of heroic bravery, anniversary celebrations, criminal court cases, military service, student spotlights, business advertisements, and so much more.  The stories found within can add a depth of color and interest to your ancestors lives that can’t be found anywhere else, and may even include photographs. </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The most popular tool for genealogy research is likely the subscription based database <a href="https://www.newspapers.com/">Newspapers.com</a>, and it’s a great tool if you are willing to pay. Use it as a first stop, and then expand your research for added results.  Many newspaper websites may have their own archive available online. You can often find copies of historical newspapers at local libraries, in county courthouses, in state archives, and in local historical and genealogical societies. </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Look up the newspapers that were in business at the time and places your ancestor lived. City directories are useful here, or a quick web search. There’s also a <a href="https://chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/search/titles/">directory from the Library of Congress </a>that can help you figure this out. Look at the state, county, city, town, and neighborhood levels. Keep in mind many places had several competing newspapers in the same area, and each of these papers ran a variety of schedules like the daily paper, the Sunday paper, or the evening paper.  Also think about societies that have published their own newspapers and may have current archives, like colleges or universities, various ethnic or religious groups, or military branches. </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">When researching newspapers, you can search for events specific to an individual’s life by using variations of their name, or you can browse local papers during specific time periods for relevant information. </p><p class="" data-rte-preserve-empty="true" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"></p><h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>Newspaper Subscription or Pay Sites</strong></h3><ul data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://www.newspapers.com/">Newspapers.com</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://newspaperarchive.com/">NewspaperArchive.com</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://paperofrecord.hypernet.ca/default.asp">Paper of Record</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://www.readex.com/collections">Readex World Newspaper Archive</a></p></li></ul><p class="" data-rte-preserve-empty="true" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"></p><h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>Free Newspaper Research Sites: </strong></h3><ul data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/">Library of Congress: Chronicling America</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://news.google.com/newspapers/">Google News Archive</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://elephind.com/">Elephind.com </a>- search world newspapers</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://archive.org/">The Internet Archive (Archive.org)</a> -
