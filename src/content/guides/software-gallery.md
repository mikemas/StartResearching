<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">FamilyTree Maker - “FTM” is a download, DVD, or USB. Windows or Mac. Can connect with Ancestry.com or FamilySearch.org. Recently changed ownership but still supported.  <a href="https://www.mackiev.com/ftm/" target="">mackiev.com/ftm</a></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Windows only program, more than 30 years old. <a href="https://www.bkwin.org/" target="">bkwin.org</a></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Ancestral Quest Basics: Free version with limited function available, or full Ancestral Quest download, now version 16 Free upgrade from previous version.. Over 25 years old. Syncs data with FamilySearch Family Tree. <a href="https://www.ancquest.com/index.htm" target="">ancquest.com</a></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Free, open-source software download developed for Linux and UNIX-like operating systems but also available for Windows and Mac. <a href="https://gramps-project.org/blog/" target="">gramps-project.org</a></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Windows only. Standard edition free download, deluxe edition extra. Over 22 years old, now on version 9. Offers multiple languages on interface. <a href="https://legacyfamilytree.com/" target="">legacyfamilytree.com</a></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Now called Family Historian 6. Windows only Free 30 day trial, then costs to upgrade.  <a href=".https://www.family-historian.co.uk/" target="">.family-historian.co.uk</a></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Family Tree Builder, from MyHeritage. Free download with limited functions, requires MyHeritage subscription for most searches. Can download and / or use online. Integrates with MyHeritage DNA kit. offers a variety of languages in user interface. <a href="https://www.myheritage.com/family-tree-builder" target="">myheritage.com/family-tree-builder</a></p>
<p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">“Roots Magic Essentials” free limited version download of full program “Roots Magic 7”.  Windows and Mac. Over 17 years old. Syncs with Ancestry and some parts of FamilySearch.  <a href="https://www.rootsmagic.com/" target="">rootsmagic.com</a></p>
