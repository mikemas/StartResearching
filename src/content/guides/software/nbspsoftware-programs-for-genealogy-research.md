<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content>
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A <strong>software program</strong> is bought and downloaded on to your computer, either through physical media like a CD / DVD or USB device, or through an app that you download. This is different from online databases like FamilySearch.org and subscription based services like Ancestry.com, that you use through your computer’s web browser.&nbsp; </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Genealogy software, specifically, can be used to record, organize, preserve, and publish your research. Some programs also allow searching for records.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The use of these software programs is completely optional and up to your preference. You should use whatever you are comfortable with to organize and store your information. Maybe it’s a software program like FamilyTree Maker, an online database like Ancestry.com, a collection of handwritten charts and notebooks, or some combination of all of these.</p><h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A good genealogy software program should be able to: </h3><ul data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Create a family tree (ideally options to create multiple family trees) </p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Record vital information about your ancestors, showing relationships between them, and document the sources for this information</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Upload documents and store them, as well as other media like photos and video files. </p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Give the option for you to list sources and notes about these documents and media</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Create and print charts or reports</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Offer you hints and provide tools to further your research</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Protect your research with backups, either manually or automatic</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Easily export your information and family tree to your computer in a widely used format, like GEDCOM (file type that ends with .ged extension) </p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Possibly sync with online databases, such as FamilySearch or Ancestry, to allow searching for records </p></li></ul><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><br>Of course not all programs offer all of the above features. Think about what it is you hope to accomplish, and find out if the software you plan to use can help you get there. </p><p class="" data-rte-preserve-empty="true" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"></p><h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Some genealogy software options: </h3>
