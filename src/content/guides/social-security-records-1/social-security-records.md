  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The Social Security Act was created in 1935 to provide retirement benefits for older Americans. In 1939 the Act expanded to include spouses and minor children of retired or deceased workers. It would later help workers who became disabled. When medicare became law in 1965 many people over age 65 applied for social security numbers to receive benefits. </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">When you work, you pay taxes into Social Security based on your earnings (up to a certain amount), and receive the benefits when you retire, if you become disabled, or if you are a survivor or dependent of a worker who died. Your Social Security Number (SSN) is used to link your earnings and benefits.</p><ul data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Puckett, Carolyn.<a href="https://www.ssa.gov/policy/docs/ssb/v69n2/v69n2p55.html#:~:text=P.L.&amp;text=The%20Social%20Security%20number%20(%20SSN%20)%20was%20created%20in%201936%20for,entitlement%20and%20computing%20benefit%20levels.&amp;text=Today%20the%20SSN%20may%20be,system%20in%20the%20United%20States."><em>The Story of the Social Security Number.</em></a><em> </em>Social Security Bulletin, Vol. 69, No. 2, 2009. ssa.gov</p></li></ul><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">There are three main resources best for genealogy provided by the Social Security Administration: </p><ol data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>”Social Security Death Index” or SSDI</strong> reports people that have had social security numbers, and whose deaths have been reported to the administration, beginning with deaths reported in 1962.  </p><ul data-rte-list="default"><li><p The Area number, or first three numbers, were assigned geographically until 2011 (when they began assigning them randomly). Before 1972 it was based on the state where the card was issued, and after 1972 it was based on the zip code of the mailing address on the application. For example, someone with the first three digits of a SSN of 001, 002, or 003 would have either applied for a social security card in the state of New Hampshire or had a NH mailing address on their application (prior to 2011). </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The Group Number, or second two numbers, were assigned odd numbers 01 through 09, then even numbers 10 through 98, followed by even groups 02 through 08, then odd groups 11 through 99. </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The Serial Number, or last four numbers, were assigned in sequence starting with 0001 except every fifth number was chosen from 2001 - 2999 or 7001 -<em> </em>7999. Serial number 0000 is never used. Again, this was until 2011 when the change to randomization occurred. </p><p class="" data-rte-preserve-empty="true" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"></p><h3 style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Sources for above: </h3><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The SSN Numbering Scheme - <a href="https://www.ssa.gov/history/ssn/geocard.html">ssa.gov/history/ssn/geocard.html</a></p><p class="" data-rte-preserve-empty="true" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"></p>
